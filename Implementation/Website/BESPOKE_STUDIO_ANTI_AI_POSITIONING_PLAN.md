# Bespoke Studio & Anti-AI Positioning Master Plan
**Mercian Wealth — High-Trust UK Engineering & Automation Studio**

---

## 1. Executive Vision & Philosophy
The objective is to establish **Mercian Wealth** as the premier automation and custom web engineering partner for local UK businesses, deliberately avoiding both the generic "AI-generated SaaS template" syndrome and traditional static brochure agencies that saturate the market.

Mercian Wealth combines **bespoke editorial elegance** with **tangible interactive tooling, 24/7 upfront deposit collection, and transparent pricing mechanics**.

## 2. The 5 Anti-AI Design & Copy Shifts

```
┌──────────────────────────────┬───────────────────────────────┬──────────────────────────────────────────┐

│ Localized Trust Signals      │ Vague global identity         │ Direct UK phone, WhatsApp & city regions │
└──────────────────────────────┴───────────────────────────────┴──────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Pillar 1: Language Shift from Sci-Fi Jargon to Operational Reality
* **De-emphasize / Eliminate**:
  * Abstract terms like *"Autonomic Multi-Agent Ingestion Protocol"*, *"Deep Cognitive Synapses"*, or *"Nexus Ingestion"*.
* **Emphasize**:
  * Concrete daily operator realities:
    * *"Missed weekend orders while your hands are covered in flour."*
    * *"Late-night quote chasing on WhatsApp instead of resting."*
    * *"3-tap automated mobile storefronts with 50% upfront deposits locked into Stripe before work begins."*

### Pillar 2: Editorial Studio Aesthetic (Mayfair / Savile Row Feel)
* **Canvas**: Canonical Deep Navy (`#020E28`) sampled directly from `public/MercianWealthLogo.jpeg`.
* **Surfaces**: Solid Elevated Navy (`#07153B` and `#0C1D4D`) with crisp 1px borders (`rgba(218, 166, 64, 0.20)`).
* **Accents**: Canonical Gold (`#DAA640`) and Warm Gold Text Highlight (`#EBB755`), both verified WCAG AAA (8.06:1 to 10.43:1 contrast).
* **Typography**: Clean, confident typography with tabular numeric alignment for metrics (`tnum`).

### Pillar 3: Tangible Interactive Artifacts
* **Live Interactive ROI Engine**: Responsive sliders calculating real admin hours saved (£25/hr labor valuation) and automated deposit capture lift (+18%).
* **Interactive WhatsApp Order Simulator**: Realistic mobile device preview displaying real-time lead capture, deposit confirmation, and webhook dispatch.
* **Portfolio Sandbox Badging**: Clear demarcation between `Interactive Sandbox · Live Preview` (fully interactive deployed builds) and `Architecture Case Study`.

### Pillar 4: Founder Credibility & "Anti-Agency" Positioning
* **The "Anti-Agency" Stance**:
  > *"Traditional agencies charge £5,000 for a static brochure website that sits there waiting for traffic. We engineer automated revenue engines that actively capture customer specifications and collect deposits 24/7."*
* **Direct Access**:
  * Visible UK contact phone: `+44 7851 055929`
  * Direct 1-tap WhatsApp dispatch
  * Founder diagnostic calendar booking

### Pillar 5: Transparent Pricing as a Competitive Weapon
* **The Conventional Agency Flaw**: Hidden pricing requiring endless back-and-forth email friction.
* **Mercian Wealth Advantage**: Published transparent starting tiers (One-Time Setup from £495 / Monthly Growth Retainer from £99–£195/mo) with complete feature matrices.
